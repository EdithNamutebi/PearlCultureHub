Our story

About Pearl Cultural Hub:

This culture hub was born from a desire to create a home where Ugandan culture isn't just displayed but lived, where the dancer, the story teller, the artisan, they all have a voice.
We started this so the world could feel what we feel, that in Uganda culture isn't in museums, its in the air, the soil, the hands and the hearts.

Our Misson

To connect with authentic Ugandan culture through experiences, crafts and story telling while empowering local artisans and culture practioners

Our Vision

A world where Uganda's rich heritage is celebrated, preserved and shared across generations and borders

What we Offer

  • Cultural experience and tours
  • Authentic handcrafts
  • Traditional Dance & Music
  • Story telling and education
